Vacuum bag opener

A compact, table-mounted concept for your 15 × 9 × 28 cm kraft-paper bag, placed between Shelf 1 and Shelf 2. The opposing plates are inferred from the supplied video; the actuator, cup layout and dimensions are proposed.

The fixture uses 36 cm along the counter and 27 cm of its 35 cm depth. That leaves approximately 4 cm at its front and rear when centred. The clear distance between the rack frames is about 105 cm.

Eight provisional Ø20 mm cups: four on a fixed plate and four on a guided moving plate. Two independently monitored vacuum circuits retain the opposite paper walls.

The CAD height envelope is 33 cm. The example bag bottom sits 4.4 cm above the 90 cm counter.

The limiting dimension is the 9 cm bag opening. Current bread placeholders are 10.2 × 9.4 cm in plan, exceeding the modelled rectangular opening in one direction before allowing room for the tongs. Actual buns, bag flexibility and the complete gripper envelope must be checked. A tilted insertion or a wider bag may be necessary.

Proposed operating sequence

  1. Present one flat bag against the rear plate, registered on the bottom support. This first concept assumes manual or robot feeding of individual bags.
  2. Close the moving plate, engage both vacuum circuits, and confirm paper contact.
  3. Retract the front plate about 78 mm. Verify the actual mouth opening before allowing a robot to insert food.
  4. Keep the bag supported and held open while the robot loads it.
  5. Release vacuum, then remove the bag for closing and handoff.

Automatic blank singulation, sealing and receipt application are separate tasks and are not modelled as solved functions here.

No joint-limit, tool-orientation, mobile-base docking, collision or trajectory validation has been performed.

Feasible to prototype

Vacuum opening is an established packaging technique; Piab describes suction cups for opening bags before filling. Their bag-handling guidance also makes clear that cup design and vacuum flow need to suit the material.

  • Test your real kraft paper, seams, folds and finish for leaks and double-wall pickup.
  • Choose cups and vacuum supply from measured seal/leak behaviour; the 20 mm cups shown are provisional.
  • Use a mechanical bottom support, vacuum confirmation, opening confirmation, and a recoverable failed-open state.
  • Check bun-plus-tong insertion and release inside the 90 mm nominal depth.
  • Keep crumbs out of the slide and make the tray and cups removable for cleaning. Guard the closing plate’s pinch area.

Start with one bag size and single-bag feeding. Prove repeatable opening and loading before adding a magazine, sealing or receipt handling.

Editable source

The standalone dimensions and bag settings are in parameters.json; its geometry is generated by the shared bag_opener.py. The full layout’s corresponding object is bag_opener in scene_config.json. The editor supports moving, rotating, scaling, hiding and exporting the fixture together with the scene.
