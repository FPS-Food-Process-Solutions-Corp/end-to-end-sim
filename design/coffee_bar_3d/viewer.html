Controls & scene notes

Shift-click to select multiple items. Space-drag pans the plan. Alt disables snapping; Shift snaps rotation to 15°.

Arrow keys nudge 1 cm; Shift nudges 10 cm. Ctrl C copies, Ctrl V pastes, Ctrl D duplicates, Ctrl Z undoes, Delete removes. Copying a table or cart includes its mounted equipment.

Position is the object's centre measured from the plan's top-left. Rotation is clockwise. Width and depth follow the object's local axes. Use Resize settings above the views to choose the fixed side and whether mounted child objects stay in place. Drag an edge handle to resize just width or depth. Corner handles resize both; Shift keeps proportions. Robot and round dispenser models scale proportionally.

Distances are horizontal plan distances. Reach circles are planning envelopes, not a collision or joint-limit simulation.

Save SVG or JSON to reopen an editable scene. GLB exports current visible geometry; Blender downloads the original generated layout.
